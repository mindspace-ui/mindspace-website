SMALL STEPS COUNT

Advice for everyday well-being

You don't need a perfect routine. Try one small thing that feels possible.

☘

Make room for rest

Take short breaks between tasks. A pause is part of caring for yourself, not a failure to be productive.

☏

Reach out

Share what you're feeling with a trusted friend, family member, teacher, or counselor when you need support.

☼

Keep it simple

Drink water, stretch if it feels comfortable, get some fresh air, or do one manageable task.

♡

Talk kindly to yourself

Replace “I should be doing better” with “I'm learning, and I can take this one step at a time.”
